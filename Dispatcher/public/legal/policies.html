Privacy Policy

We are a small shuttle and freight operator in northern Manitoba. We collect the information needed to carry you safely, to bill correctly, and to meet the records we are legally required to keep as a licensed carrier. Nothing more. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

1Who we are, and who to contact

Northern Link Shuttle & Cargo is a sole proprietorship based in Leaf Rapids, Manitoba, Canada, operating scheduled passenger service, charters and parcel freight in northern Manitoba.

Emelio Campbell, the owner, is the person accountable for personal information under this policy. Any question, access request or complaint about privacy comes to him directly.

2What this policy covers

This policy applies to three pieces of software and to the booking records behind them:

  • The Northern Link passenger app (iOS and Android), used by members of the public to view the schedule, book seats and parcels, pay, and follow a shuttle in progress.
  • The Northern Link driver app (Android tablet), used by our own drivers to complete trip inspections, manage manifests, record hours and report defects.
  • The Trip Creator client portal (web), used by corporate and institutional clients to request trips and view their own trip history and invoices.

It also covers bookings taken by phone, text or email, because those records sit in the same system.

4Why we collect it

  • To carry you. We need a name to put on a manifest, a phone number to reach you if a run changes, and a pickup point to collect you from.
  • To take payment and issue receipts.
  • To tell you what is happening. Booking confirmations, departure and arrival notices, and warnings about weather or road closures on Highway 391.
  • To operate safely. Trip inspections, hours of service and journey check-ins exist so that a vehicle on a remote highway is roadworthy and someone always knows where it is.
  • To meet the law. As a licensed carrier holding a Manitoba Safety Fitness Certificate, we are required to create and keep specific records and to produce them to a peace officer or auditor on request. Section 9 sets out how long each is kept.

We rely on your consent for anything beyond these purposes, and you can withdraw it at any time. Withdrawing consent for notifications will not affect your bookings.

5Payments

Card payments in the passenger app are processed by Square, including Apple Pay and Google Pay where your device supports them. Your card number, expiry and security code go directly to Square and are handled under their security standards.

We never see or store your full card number. What we receive back is a record that a payment succeeded, the amount, and the last four digits so you and we can identify the transaction on a statement.

If you pay by e-transfer or cash, no card information exists at all. We keep only the record of the amount received and the booking it applies to.

6Location information

There are two different kinds of location data in our system and they are worth separating clearly.

The vehicle's location. While a trip is running, the driver app broadcasts the position of the shuttle. This is what lets you see on a map how far away your ride is, and it is what lets us find a vehicle that has stopped reporting on a remote stretch of road. It is the vehicle being tracked, not you.

The driver's location while on duty. For our drivers, vehicle position during a shift is also a record of where that driver was. We collect it because it is the backbone of journey management on a 322 km run with patchy cell coverage, and because it supports the hours of service and trip records we are required to keep. It is recorded only while a driver is on duty and the app is in a working state. It is not collected on personal time, and it is not used to monitor anyone off shift.

The passenger app does not need your location. It will ask for location permission only if you choose a feature that uses it, and declining does not stop you booking, paying or travelling. You can withdraw the permission in your device settings at any time.

8Where information is stored

Our records are kept on servers operated by our cloud hosting providers, located in Canada and the United States. Several of the service providers listed above are United States companies.

This means your information may be stored or processed outside Canada, and while it is in another country it may be accessible to that country's courts, law enforcement and national security authorities under that country's laws. We use providers that commit to protecting it under contract, but we cannot override those laws.

Our business records, as required of a licensed carrier, are kept at our chief place of business in Manitoba and can be produced there.

10Passenger lists uploaded by clients

Corporate and institutional clients using the Trip Creator portal upload lists of the people they are sending on a trip. We hold that information to build the manifest and carry those passengers.

If you appear on such a list, it was given to us by the organisation arranging your travel, not collected from you. That organisation is responsible for having the authority to share it with us, and for telling you that it has. We use it only to carry you and to keep the manifest records we are required to keep, and we will answer an access request from you directly.

If a client uploads a list to us

You are responsible for ensuring you may lawfully share those names and contact details with us, and for informing the people on the list. Please upload only what the trip requires — a name is enough.

11Children and young people

Our apps are not designed for or directed at children, and we do not knowingly create accounts for anyone under 18. An account must be held by an adult.

Young people do travel with us, and our conditions of carriage govern that: passengers under 12 must travel with an adult, and passengers aged 12 to 17 may travel only with a signed consent form from a parent or guardian. Where we hold a young person's name on a manifest or a consent form, it was provided by their parent, guardian or school, and we use it only to carry them safely and to keep the manifest.

If you believe a child has created an account or given us information directly, contact us and we will delete it.

12Your rights

Under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, you may:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you and how we have used and disclosed it.
  • Get a copy of it.
  • Have it corrected if it is wrong.
  • Withdraw consent, subject to the records we are legally required to keep and to any booking already in progress.
  • Ask us to delete what we are not required to keep.
  • Complain about how we have handled it.

Email or phone us using the contact details in section 1. We will respond within 30 days. There is no charge for a reasonable request. We may need to verify who you are before releasing information, which protects you as much as us.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca.

13Security

Information travels encrypted between the apps and our servers, and is stored encrypted. Accounts require a password, and access to operational records is limited to people who need it to do their work — a driver sees the manifest for their own trips, not the whole customer base.

We are a small operation and we do not pretend to run a security department. What we do is keep the amount of information we hold small, keep card data out of our hands entirely, and use established providers for the parts that matter most. If a breach ever occurs that creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we will tell you and report it as the law requires.

14What we don't do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. Not to anyone, for any price.
  • We do not use it for advertising, and we carry no third-party advertising or analytics trackers for ad targeting.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not build profiles of you, and we make no automated decisions about you.
  • We do not share your travel history with your employer, unless your employer booked and paid for that specific trip.

We will tell you as early as we can and you will not be charged for a run we cancel. We are not able to cover onward costs such as missed appointments or connections — section 11 explains the limit.
